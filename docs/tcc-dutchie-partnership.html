Twin City Cannabis
Integration Brief Prepared for Dutchie

Bringing Dutchie storefronts into Minnesota's first comprehensive cannabis price-comparison layer.

Twin City Cannabis is the consumer-facing utility for shopping cannabis in Minnesota. We index every licensed dispensary in the state, refresh menus and prices multiple times a day, and surface comparisons by category, city, brand, and strain. About half the market is fully searchable today. Dutchie-powered shops are the missing piece.

What we'd do with the data
  • Index Dutchie storefronts in real time alongside the Weedmaps, Jane, and Carrot sources we already pull. Same refresh cadence (every few hours), same surfaces (product, brand, strain, and city comparison pages).
  • Deep-link every product card back to the dispensary's own Dutchie storefront for checkout. TCC stays consumer-facing only. No resale, no order routing, no checkout intercepting.
  • Flag Dutchie operators in our dispensary detail pages with accurate menus and prices, ending the empty-menu state visitors currently see.

Why this matters now. Minnesota's adult-use market opened in 2024 and is still volatile on pricing and supply. Consumers are actively asking for comparison tools, and operators are looking for low-cost discovery channels. The shops most easily indexed (Weedmaps, Jane, Carrot) are winning visibility right now. Bringing Dutchie into that comparison evens the playing field for Dutchie's operators and gives Minnesota shoppers the comprehensive view they're looking for.

Technical readiness

Stack

  • Cloudflare Workers + KV for the API layer and dashboard
  • Raspberry Pi cron running Python scrapers on a 4×/day schedule
  • Static GitHub Pages frontend, ~570 prerendered pages, ~3,200 products
  • Existing integrations: Weedmaps, Jane, Carrot Cannabis

Posture

  • Independent. Free for consumers and dispensaries. No ads.
  • No paid placement in organic rankings. Ever.
  • Full data refresh audit trail via git commit history
  • Privacy: no PII collected on anonymous visitors
