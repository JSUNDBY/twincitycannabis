Minnesota cannabis price trends: 5 months of weekly data

Updated 2026-09-03. Twin City Cannabis has recorded listed prices on every menu refresh since March 30, 2026 — 6,207 products feed these weekly medians. The headline: Minnesota cannabis prices are strikingly stable. The market's growth has shown up as more selection, not lower shelf prices.

Flower (3.5g eighth)

The median 3.5g eighth has held at $50 essentially every week since March 2026 — from 90 eighths tracked then to 385 now. Competition so far has widened the range (the cheapest eighths sit well under $30) without moving the middle.

Vape cartridges

Cartridges are the one category that moves: the weekly median has swung between $55 and $70, ending the latest week at $70. The swings track market composition — new stores and brands entering — more than repricing of existing products.

Edibles

The median edible has not moved from $20 in 5 months, across a 5x growth in tracked products (236 → 1185). The $20 gummy bag is Minnesota's most stable cannabis price point.

Pre-rolls

Pre-rolls drifted from about $17 up to $18 through early summer and back to $17 — the only category to round-trip. Range: $16.24–$18.00.

How to read this honestly

These are listed menu prices, before Minnesota's roughly 17–18% cannabis and sales taxes. Coverage grew from Twin Cities metro shops in spring to statewide (including RISE and Green Goods) by late August, so later weeks rest on much larger samples; each product counts once per week no matter how often it was observed. Gaps in the lines are weeks with no recorded data. The underlying medians recompute as the tracker runs, updated daily.
