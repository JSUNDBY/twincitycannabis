Frequently asked questions

What is the best cannabis dispensary in Minneapolis?

It depends on what matters to you — price, brand selection, atmosphere, or location. Twin City Cannabis ranks every metro dispensary by real Google ratings and pulls live menus, so you can compare both vibe and value side by side.

How does Twin City Cannabis rank dispensaries?

The ranking on this page is straight Google Maps ratings and review counts — no editorial picks, no paid placement. Free listings are ranked exactly the same as Featured listings.

What is the difference between a Featured and a free dispensary listing?

Featured listings get a banner ad placement and priority in our directory; ranking on this page is unaffected. Featured shops do not get a TCC Score boost or better visibility in price comparisons.

Are all licensed Minnesota dispensaries on Twin City Cannabis?

Yes — every dispensary licensed by the Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management is in our directory, and new shops are added within days of opening.

How can I tell if a Minnesota dispensary is legitimate?

Check that they hold a current OCM (Office of Cannabis Management) license — every shop in our directory is verified against the official OCM list. Avoid grey-market vendors selling cannabis without a license.

What is the TCC Score?

A 0-100 score derived from a shop’s Google customer rating and review volume. Higher star ratings and more reviewers lift the score; unrated or brand-new shops start at 70 by default. It is meant as a quick credibility signal, not a comprehensive review.

Do Twin Cities dispensaries take credit cards?

Most are cash-only or use cashless ATM systems where you swipe a debit card, the system rounds up to the nearest five dollars in cash, and you hand the change back to the cashier. A few accept debit directly. Always check ahead.

Can I order ahead at Minneapolis cannabis dispensaries?

Many shops let you place an order online via Dutchie, Jane, or their own platform and pick up in store. Each dispensary detail page on TCC links straight to its online ordering system when one exists.

What ID do I need to buy cannabis at a Twin Cities dispensary?

A government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. Out-of-state IDs are accepted; tribal IDs and active-duty military IDs work too. Expired IDs are not accepted.

When do dispensaries close in the Twin Cities?

Most metro dispensaries are open 9-10 AM to 8-10 PM, but hours vary by shop. Check the specific dispensary detail page for current hours before you drive.

What if a dispensary listing on Twin City Cannabis is wrong?

Owners can claim their listing to correct it. Shoppers who notice prices or details that look off can email hello@twincitycannabis.com — we fix it within a day.

Can I trust the prices listed on Twin City Cannabis?

Yes — prices are scraped four times a day directly from each dispensary’s live menu. The timestamp at the top of every comparison page shows the last update. Always confirm before you drive, since stock can move quickly.
