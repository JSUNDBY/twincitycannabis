Blaine dispensaries: three shops, and the cheapest ounce in Minnesota

Three licensed shops, one of them running an ounce that ties for the cheapest in Minnesota. Here is who wins which shelf, and which prices you need to call and check.

Blaine has three licensed dispensaries, which is more than most suburbs its size. One of them publishes a deep menu we can read every day, one publishes nothing at all, and the third comes and goes. When all three are visible they disagree sharply on price, and the shop that is cheapest on flower is not the shop that is cheapest on anything else. That is the whole reason to read this before you go.

One thing to know up front, because it changes how you should read every number below. Blaine Dispensary's menu drops out of our feed for weeks at a time and then comes back. Any figure we give for that shop is its last published menu, not a promise about today, so call before you drive on the strength of it. Green Goods publishes daily and its numbers re-price themselves on this page several times a day.

The three shops

Green Goods Blaine, 672 County Highway 10, (763) 400-4777, 10am to 8pm. Medical and adult-use, part of the eight location Green Goods chain. It has 955 Google reviews at a 4.4 average, the most reviews and the lowest rating of the three, which usually just means it has been open the longest. Deepest menu in town at 222 priced listings.

Blaine Dispensary, 1536 NE 125th Avenue, (763) 703-4202, 10am to 9pm, 90 reviews at a 4.8 average. Independent, online ordering, and open latest in town. Its menu went dark on us earlier this fall and has come back, over 100 listings deep.

Loon Leaf, 8700 University Ave NE, (763) 215-8828, 10am to 6pm, 140 reviews at a 4.9 average. Best rated shop in Blaine and the one we have never been able to price. It closes at six, earlier than anywhere else in town, so it is the easiest one to accidentally drive to after work and find shut.

Geography matters. Green Goods and Loon Leaf are less than a mile apart on the west side near Highway 10. Blaine Dispensary is about six miles east. All three are on our Blaine dispensary page.

Flower: $41.89 against $60, in the same suburb

Green Goods runs a median eighth of $41.89 across 24 listings. Blaine Dispensary runs $60 across eighteen. The median eighth statewide is $50, so one of these shops sits well under the market and the other sits well over it, and they are a ten minute drive apart.

If flower is the errand, that is not a close call, and the gap is wide enough to be worth crossing town for. Live numbers are on cheapest flower in Blaine, and the reason not to shop by the THC number on the label is in the THC percentage myth.

The cheapest ounce in the state is sitting in Blaine

This is the find. Green Goods Blaine's size ladder runs $41.89 for an eighth, $70 for a seven gram, $120 for a half ounce and $220 for a full ounce.

Per gram that is $11.97, then $10.00, then $8.57, then $7.86. That last number ties for the lowest ounce price at any dispensary in Minnesota we can price, across every shop with enough ounce listings to measure. Buying one ounce instead of eight separate eighths cuts almost a third off what you pay per gram, which is a steeper volume discount than most Minnesota shops offer at any size.

One honest asterisk. Green Goods charges the same $220 at all eight of its Minnesota locations, so this is a chain price rather than a Blaine price. Nobody should drive across the metro for it. The point is that if you already live up here, the cheapest flower in the state by volume is a few minutes away.

Only buy the ounce if you will actually get through it, which means reading the storage guide first, especially heading into furnace season.

Then the two shops swap places

Here is the part that makes Blaine worth shopping properly instead of defaulting to the cheap flower. On most of what is not flower, Blaine Dispensary is the better buy.

  • Edibles: Blaine Dispensary, 60 listings at a $19.50 median against Green Goods' 56 at $28. The state median is $20, so one shop is at the market and the other runs well over it.
  • Drinks: Blaine Dispensary, ten listings at a $6.99 median, right at the $7 state middle. Green Goods barely stocks the shelf at all.
  • Tinctures: Blaine Dispensary, three listings at a $20 median. Green Goods runs a $94 median against $40 statewide, which is more than double the middle of the market.
  • Cartridges: A wash. Blaine Dispensary lists six at a $70 median, Green Goods 37 at $62.83. Both sit over the $65 state middle, so neither is a bargain.
  • Pre-rolls: Green Goods, the one non-flower category it wins. 26 listings at a $15 median against Blaine Dispensary's seventeen at $17, which is exactly the state median.

Green Goods' concentrates land at a $65 median against $65 statewide, so that shelf is a wash rather than a reason to go. Blaine Dispensary lists none. What you are actually buying in each of those categories is in the tincture guide, the drinks guide, the cartridge guide and the pre-roll guide.

The shop we still cannot price

Loon Leaf publishes nothing we can read, and we are not going to guess. Call them. Its 4.9 across 140 reviews is the best rating in Blaine by a clear margin and people do not leave that score for a shop that overcharges them, but a rating is not a price.

Worth knowing how fast this moves. Blaine Dispensary's entire menu vanished from our data this fall and came back within weeks, which is why the shape of this town looks different from month to month. Any price quoted for a shop whose menu has gone dark, ours included, deserves suspicion until the menu returns. If Loon Leaf starts publishing, it will show up on the Blaine page within a day.

Ten minutes gets you a lot more

Blaine sits in the densest cluster of dispensaries outside Minneapolis. Fridley is under two miles south with two shops, Anoka is seven miles west, and Brooklyn Park is five miles southwest with Urban Green and a RISE, which we covered in the Brooklyn Park guide. Mounds View and New Brighton each add one within a few miles.

Twenty of the dispensaries we track sit within ten miles of Blaine, so comparison shopping costs you almost nothing up here. That is where the deals page, open now and the cheapest cannabis tracker earn their keep, and it is the opposite of the two shop towns where you take what you get.

The short version

Flower and pre-rolls at Green Goods, and buy the ounce if you will genuinely finish it, because it ties for the cheapest in Minnesota. Most of the rest at Blaine Dispensary, which is cheaper on edibles, drinks and tinctures, with carts a wash. Call Loon Leaf rather than trusting any published price for it, ours included. Check the Blaine page the morning you go, and the tax calculator before you decide what you can afford.
