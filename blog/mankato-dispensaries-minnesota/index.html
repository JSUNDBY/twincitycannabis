Mankato dispensaries: a $20 gap on an eighth, and a size ladder that pays all the way up

Two shops you can walk into, and the cheaper one depends entirely on what you came for. Flower at one, edibles at the other, and a size ladder where every step up genuinely pays.

Mankato has a state university, roughly 45,000 people, and three cannabis listings on our map. Two of them are places you can actually walk into. The price gap between those two is one of the widest we have found in any city this size, and the surprise is that it runs in opposite directions depending on what is on your list.

The two shops, and the third listing

RISE Dispensary Mankato, 1 Sioux Rd Suite 100, (507) 405-0369, Monday through Saturday 9am to 9pm and Sunday 10am to 8pm. Medical and adult-use, part of the statewide RISE chain. It has 1,351 Google reviews at a 4.8 average, which makes it the most reviewed dispensary anywhere south of the Twin Cities, by roughly double the next one down.

Voyager Cannabis Co, 1889 Madison Avenue, (507) 779-7013. A microbusiness, newly licensed, 70 reviews at a 4.8 average. Our data lists its hours as check website, which is our way of saying they move around, so call before you drive over.

There is a third entry on our Mankato dispensary page, Crafty Canna Mist LLC on Sioux Road. It shares Voyager's phone number and website, so it reads as the same operation under its license name rather than a third storefront. Shop Mankato as a two shop town.

Flower: a $20 gap on the same unit

This is the headline. The eighths we have on file at RISE Mankato run a median of $45 across 39 listings, from $25 up to $55. Voyager's eighths sit at a median of $65, from $55 up to $70, though that is only 5 listings deep so treat it as a direction rather than a precise number.

The median eighth across the 144 dispensaries we track in Minnesota is $50. So RISE sits just under the state middle and Voyager runs well over it, in the same city. If flower is the errand, that is not a close call. Live numbers are on cheapest flower in Mankato.

Every step up the ladder pays here

This is the most useful arithmetic on the page. At RISE the ladder runs $45 for an eighth, a median of $80 for a seven gram, $150 for a half ounce and $275 for a full ounce.

Per gram that is $12.86 at the eighth, $11.43 at the quarter, $10.71 at the half ounce and $9.82 at the ounce. Every number on that list is smaller than the one above it, by about a dollar a gram each time. The biggest single saving is the first step up, from an eighth to a quarter, which is also the smallest commitment on the ladder.

From there it keeps paying, but you are buying a lot of flower to collect it. The full ounce runs about three dollars a gram under the eighth, close to a quarter off, and it is also eight times as much flower sitting in your house through furnace season. Buy the size you will actually finish, not the size with the best per-gram number. The general version is in how to actually save money, and the keeping-it-fresh half is in the storage guide.

One warning, and we mean it. This ladder moves. The quarter here has swung between $70 and $80 inside a month, which is enough to change which step is the smart one, and the half ounce and ounce listings are thin enough that a single new jar shifts the median. Do the division on the day, with the shelf in front of you, rather than trusting this paragraph.

Voyager owns the edible shelf

Flip the story and Voyager wins. Its edibles run a median of $15, from $3 to $60. RISE's run a median of $20, from $6 to $120. The state median is $20.

Topicals go the same way. Voyager's run from $5 to $10 against a $40 median at RISE. A microbusiness making its own product can price like that and a national chain working through distribution cannot. If gummies or a balm are the reason you are going out, the trip to Madison Avenue pays for itself. Current numbers are on cheapest edibles in Mankato, and our edibles dosing guide covers how to actually take them.

Drinks are a wash. Both shops land near the $7 state median, but RISE lists 8 against Voyager's 2, so selection goes to RISE. Our THC drinks guide has what to look for on the label.

Carts and pre-rolls, shop by shop

RISE has 33 cartridges at a $60 median, against a $60 state middle. Voyager has 6, all between $70 and $75. So the cheaper cart in this town is at RISE Dispensary Mankato, and Voyager's handful is a thin shelf to judge a shop on. Either way, ask whether it is a half or a full gram before you ask anything else, for the reasons in the cartridge guide.

Pre-rolls are the odd category. RISE lists 68 of them at a median of $25 and Voyager 8 at $20, against a $17 median statewide. Both shops sit above the state middle on the one thing you would expect a college town to be cheap on. Which of the two is cheaper keeps changing: it has flipped direction three times since September, and as of this build it is Voyager Cannabis Co, by $5. Look at the shelf rather than trusting this sentence. What you are actually buying is in the pre-roll guide.

You cannot price match item for item

One honest limit on all of this. Across every product we track, these two shops do not stock a single identical item. Not one. So you cannot walk in with a screenshot from the other menu and ask them to match it, the way you can with two metro independents carrying the same Grasslandz gummies.

That is why the numbers above are medians by category rather than product by product. It also tells you something real: these two are not competing on price so much as stocking different shelves in the same town.

If you are willing to drive

Eleven miles north on 169, St. Peter has Healing Harvest, 185 reviews at a 4.9 average. Twenty five miles west, New Ulm has 4 North. Neither moves the math much on its own.

Forty six miles east is Albert Lea, which has six dispensaries serving 18,000 people and all the price pressure that comes with it. We wrote that town up in the Albert Lea guide. If you are making a real trip of it, that is where the competition actually lives.

The short version

Flower at RISE, edibles and topicals at Voyager, pre-rolls at whichever one is cheaper on the day, call Voyager about hours first, and buy the biggest size you will genuinely finish, because at RISE right now every step up the ladder saves you about a dollar a gram. Prices move constantly, so look at the Mankato page the morning you go, and the tax calculator for the real out the door number.
