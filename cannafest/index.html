CannaFest questions

When is CannaFest?

The Fall Edition is Thursday, November 12, 2026, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM.

Where is CannaFest?

The Lowlands, 160 Wabasha St S, St. Paul, MN 55107.

How much are tickets?

Tickets are $40 plus tax and fees, sold by Minnesota Monthly. Use the ticket link on this page.

Is CannaFest 21+?

Yes. Guests must be 21 or older and show a valid ID to enter.

Can I buy products at CannaFest?

Yes. Minnesota Monthly says the Fall Edition adds licensed cannabis flower sales alongside shopping from 50+ vendors.

Is there on-site consumption?

Minnesota Monthly says the Fall Edition adds an outdoor area for on-site consumption, new this year, for guests 21 and older. Check cannafestmn.com for the rules before you go.

Who runs CannaFest?

Minnesota Monthly hosts CannaFest, presented by Island Peži. Twin City Cannabis is a partner and helps promote it. Tickets and any event changes come from Minnesota Monthly.
